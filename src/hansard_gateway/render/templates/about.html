About this service

This service retrieves publicly available Singapore Parliamentary Reports from the official Parliament of Singapore SPRS service on demand. It does not alter the substantive transcript text. This service is not affiliated with or operated by the Parliament of Singapore.

Source

All parliamentary content is retrieved live from the official SPRS service at sprs.parl.gov.sg. Each report page links to its official source record.

Transformation policy

HTML sanitisation and structural parsing only. No substantive text rewriting, summarisation, or paraphrase. Procedural markers, column marks, and clock marks are preserved verbatim.

Caching policy

Upstream content is cached briefly server-side (token-independent) as an optimization only. The service remains conceptually query-time retrieval.
