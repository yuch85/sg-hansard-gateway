Singapore Hansard Gateway

A gateway to the publicly available Singapore Parliamentary Reports served by the official Parliament of Singapore SPRS service. Reports are retrieved on demand and the substantive transcript text is not altered.

This service is not affiliated with or operated by the Parliament of Singapore.

Access

Retrieval requires a capability token. The public OpenAPI specification at /openapi.json documents every endpoint and its request/response shapes.

See the about page for the provenance and transformation policy.
